Important Safety Information

VISION AI is an informational assistant and is not a certified mobility aid, cane replacement, guide dog replacement, or navigation device.

  • Never rely on AI image descriptions to confirm that a path is safe, unobstructed, or free of drop-offs, steps, wet surfaces, or moving hazards.
  • Never use VISION AI to judge traffic or decide when it is safe to cross a street or road.
  • Distance and depth estimates from a single camera frame can be inaccurate; treat all descriptions as approximate visual context only.
  • Camera blur, low lighting, glare, and narrow framing can hide objects or people.
